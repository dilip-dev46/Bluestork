Why Bluestork?

A smarter way to connect people, parcels and everyday journeys. Bluestork brings customers and travellers together to make parcel delivery more convenient.

Once upon a time, a stork carried babies across distant places, bringing happiness from one person to another.

Inspired by this little fairy tale, Bluestork connects people and travellers to deliver parcels through everyday journeys.

Every journey can deliver something meaningful.

Who We Are

Bluestork is a community-driven parcel delivery platform designed to connect people who need to send parcels with travellers who are already travelling along suitable routes.

Instead of relying only on traditional delivery networks, Bluestork aims to make use of available travel journeys to support parcel transportation.

Our Mission

To make parcel delivery more accessible by connecting customers with available travellers through a simple and convenient platform.

Our Vision

To build a connected delivery community where everyday journeys can create useful delivery opportunities.

How Bluestork Works

1. Customers: Create a parcel request with pickup and delivery details.

2. Travel Partners: Share their travel routes and availability.

3. Assigner: Reviews suitable routes and assigns parcels to partners.

4. Delivery: The partner picks up and delivers the parcel while the platform tracks its progress.

What We Focus On

Convenience

Simple parcel requests and easy-to-use dashboards.

Community

Connecting customers with people already travelling.

Transparency

Clear parcel status and delivery progress.

Opportunity

Helping travellers earn through suitable parcel delivery journeys.

Our Service Area

Bluestork currently focuses on connecting destinations across Tamil Nadu, with the goal of expanding to more regions in the future.
