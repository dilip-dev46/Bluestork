Frequently Asked Questions

How does Bluestork work?

Customers create parcel requests. Available travel partners can carry parcels along their travel routes. An assigner matches suitable parcels with partners.

How can I track my parcel?

Log in to your customer account and open your parcel details to check its current delivery status.

What if my parcel is delayed?

Submit a support request under Parcel & Delivery and describe the delay.

How do travel partners receive payouts?

Partner payouts are handled manually through the Bluestork administration process.

How can I report a payment issue?

Select Payment Issues when submitting your support request. Include relevant payment details without sharing passwords or sensitive banking credentials.

Can I contact Bluestork about account problems?

Yes. Select Account Issues and explain your problem through the support form.
